Bio
Based — Hudson Valley, NY
Medium — Stoneware, single-channel video
Practice since — 2016
Represented by — Foundry Gallery

Wren Halloran works between the kiln and the camera — a thrown or hand-built form on one side, a fixed shot held past the point of comfort on the other. Trained first as a potter, the video work keeps a potter's patience: long durations, small changes, attention paid to something most people stop watching too soon.
