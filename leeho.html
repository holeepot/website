Lee Ho!

Lee Ho (Lí-hó), is the phonetic sound for “hello” in Taiwanese Hokkien, the language spoken in my parents’ home country, Taiwan. When reversed, it is my name in Mandarin Chinese, 賀禮, which means gift.
